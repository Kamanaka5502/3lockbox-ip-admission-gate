PROJECT TITLE
3LOCKBOX — Deterministic IP Admission Gate

SHORT DESCRIPTION
3LOCKBOX turns IP2Location.io geolocation and security evidence into enforceable ADMIT, REVIEW, or REFUSE consequences. It does not stop at lookup: every decision is policy-bound, replayable, and independently recorded in a tamper-evident append-only SHA-256 ledger. A gateway-facing authorization endpoint can directly return 204 for admitted traffic or 403 when a policy boundary is violated.
