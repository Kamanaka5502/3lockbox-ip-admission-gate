PROJECT TITLE
3LOCKBOX — Deterministic IP Admission Gate

SHORT DESCRIPTION
3LOCKBOX turns IP2Location.io geolocation evidence into enforceable ADMIT, REVIEW, or REFUSE decisions. Each evaluation applies a deterministic policy to IP evidence and emits a replayable SHA-256 decision receipt.
